What It Is

A CMS is a website whose content doesn't depend on a developer

A content management system gives you an admin panel where you can update text, photos, products, and pages without touching code. We choose the platform to fit your needs - WordPress for business sites and blogs, Shopify for online stores, or a custom CMS for non-standard logic.

When You Need One

Situations where a CMS is the right choice

A CMS makes sense when your content needs regular updates, or when you want to run the site yourself without a developer on call.

Editing content yourself

  • Update text and photos
  • Add new pages
  • No developer required

An online store

  • Product catalog
  • Payment and shipping
  • Order management

Blog or content marketing

  • Regular publishing
  • Categories and tags
  • SEO-friendly article structure

Migrating from an old website

  • Content migration
  • SEO preservation
  • Design refresh

A multilingual website

  • Multiple language versions
  • Flexible content management
  • Localization for new markets

A growing content team

  • Multiple editors
  • Role-based permissions
  • Scales without a rebuild
When It's Not the Fit

When a different solution makes more sense

A CMS works great for content-driven sites, but it isn't always the right tool for non-standard needs.

Complex custom logic

  • A web app with unique functionality
  • Non-standard user interaction
  • Better suited to frontend development

A single page for ads

  • No need for frequent content updates
  • Speed is the top priority
  • Better suited to a landing page

A complex internal system

  • Custom database and backend
  • Integrations with internal tools
  • Needs to be built from scratch
