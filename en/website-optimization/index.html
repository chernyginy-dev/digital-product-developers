What It Is

Optimization means a faster site without a rebuild

Website optimization works with what already exists - code, images, scripts, and technical settings. The goal is to cut load time, improve Core Web Vitals scores, and make the site easier for search engines to understand, all while keeping the design and content exactly as they are.

Core Web Vitals Google's ranking metrics

When You Need It

Situations where optimization pays off the most

If your website already works but something is holding it back from bringing in more clients, the answer is often technical.

The site loads slowly

  • Long wait for the first screen
  • Heavy images and scripts
  • Visitors leaving before it loads

Low Google rankings

  • Technical SEO errors
  • Indexing issues
  • Poor PageSpeed scores

A site built a few years ago

  • Outdated code and plugins
  • Unnecessary scripts and libraries
  • Not optimized for mobile

A high bounce rate

  • Visitors leave before it loads
  • Low conversion from ads
  • Poor mobile experience

Before an ad campaign

  • Speed affects your cost per click
  • Google factors in page speed
  • Every second impacts conversion

Before scaling your business

  • Growing traffic and load
  • Stability under pressure
  • Ready for a bigger audience
When It's Not the Fit

When a different solution makes more sense

Optimization works with what already exists. If you don't have a website yet, or need new pages, one of our other services is a better fit.

You don't have a website yet

  • Needs to be built from scratch
  • Nothing to optimize yet
  • Better suited to website development

You need a new page for an ad

  • A separate goal and offer
  • New content, not a fix
  • Better suited to a landing page

You need complex new logic

  • New functionality, not speed
  • Non-standard integrations
  • Better suited to frontend development
