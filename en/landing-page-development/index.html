What It Is

A landing page is one page built for one purpose

Unlike a multi-page website, a landing page doesn't try to tell your whole business story. Every section is built to move a visitor toward one action - submitting a form, signing up, or making a purchase. That's what makes it such an effective tool for ad campaigns, product launches, and time-limited offers.

When It's Not the Fit

When a full website makes more sense

A landing page is great for a single goal, but it isn't the right tool for every project. If your business fits one of these cases, consider full website development instead.

An online store with a catalog

  • Many products and categories
  • Cart and checkout needed
  • Order management

Content-heavy structure

  • Blog or knowledge base
  • Client login area
  • Multiple service sections

SEO across many keywords

  • Ranking for dozens of terms
  • Dedicated service pages needed
  • Long-term organic growth
